Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information when you use our website or work with Crawford Consultancy.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

At Crawford Consultancy, we take the privacy and security of personal information seriously. Whether you’re visiting our website, making an enquiry or using our services, we aim to handle your information responsibly, securely and in accordance with UK data protection law.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, how we protect it and your rights.

1. Who we are

Crawford Consultancy provides HR, payroll and business support services to small businesses. We are based in Falkirk and work with businesses across Central Scotland and beyond.

Crawford Consultancy is operated by Teri Crawford, trading as Crawford Consultancy.

For privacy enquiries, please contact:

  • Email: hello@crawfordconsultancy.co.uk
  • Website: https://crawfordconsultancy.co.uk

Where we determine how and why personal information is used for our own business activities, we act as a data controller.

When providing services that involve processing personal information on behalf of a client, we will generally act as a data processor, with the client remaining responsible for decisions about the information and how it is used.

Our responsibilities in these circumstances are set out in our service agreements and associated data protection arrangements.

2. What information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect and process:

Website enquiries and business contacts

  • Names, email addresses and telephone numbers.
  • Business names and contact details.
  • Information provided through emails, telephone calls or enquiries.
  • Details of services requested or discussed.

Our website contact form opens your email app rather than storing submissions on a server. When you send an email, your email provider processes that message under its own terms.

Client and business information

  • Client contact information and correspondence.
  • Contracts, invoices, payment records and other business administration information.
  • Information necessary to provide our agreed services.

Employee and HR information

When supporting clients with HR, payroll or business administration, we may process information relating to their employees, workers or job applicants, including:

  • Names and contact information.
  • Employment contracts and working arrangements.
  • Pay, attendance, holiday and absence information.
  • Recruitment and employment records.
  • Performance, disciplinary and grievance information.
  • Sickness absence, health and disability information where necessary.
  • Other information relevant to the services requested by the client.

Some employee information may be classed as special category personal data under UK GDPR and requires additional protection.

We only process information that is relevant and necessary for the services being provided.

Website information

Where you consent to analytics cookies, we may collect information about how our website is used, including pages visited, approximate geographical location, device type, browser information and how visitors arrive at our website.

3. How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with prospective clients.
  • Provide HR, payroll and business support services.
  • Prepare and review employment and business documentation.
  • Maintain client relationships and manage our business.
  • Issue invoices and maintain financial records.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and tax obligations.
  • Protect our legitimate business interests, including establishing, exercising or defending legal claims where necessary.
  • Understand and improve our website where visitors have consented to analytics cookies.

We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Our legal basis for processing

Where Crawford Consultancy acts as a data controller, we rely on an appropriate lawful basis under UK GDPR, depending on the circumstances. These may include:

  • Contract — where processing is necessary to enter into or fulfil a contract with an individual.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is reasonably necessary to operate our business, respond to enquiries, manage client relationships or protect our legitimate interests, provided these do not override individuals’ rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process or retain information to comply with applicable law.
  • Consent — where consent is required, including for optional website analytics cookies.

Where we act as a data processor on behalf of a client, the client is generally responsible for identifying the lawful basis for the processing and any additional conditions required for special category information. Crawford Consultancy processes that information in accordance with the client’s documented instructions and applicable data protection law.

5. Employee information and client HR services

As part of our services, clients may provide Crawford Consultancy with confidential employee information. In these circumstances, the client will generally remain the data controller and Crawford Consultancy will act as a data processor.

This means:

  • The client is responsible for ensuring personal information is collected and shared lawfully.
  • The client is responsible for providing appropriate privacy information to employees.
  • Crawford Consultancy will use the information only for the agreed services and in accordance with applicable instructions.
  • Appropriate confidentiality and security measures will be applied.
  • Information will not be disclosed to unrelated third parties without appropriate authority or another lawful reason.

Where Crawford Consultancy processes information independently for its own legal, accounting, regulatory or legitimate business purposes, it may act as a separate data controller for that processing.

Employees wishing to exercise their data protection rights in relation to their employer’s records should normally contact their employer in the first instance. Where we receive a request relating to information controlled by a client, we will handle it in accordance with our legal obligations and agreed arrangements with that client.

6. How we protect personal information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Depending on the information and the risks involved, these measures include:

  • Password-protected devices and accounts.
  • Encryption of devices and backups where appropriate.
  • Restricted access to confidential client information.
  • Secure storage and transfer arrangements.
  • Up-to-date software and security protections.
  • Confidential handling of employee and business records.
  • Secure deletion or disposal of information when no longer required.

We regularly review our security arrangements and take additional precautions when handling sensitive information, including employee health records.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Sharing information with third parties

We may use trusted service providers to support the operation of our business, including:

  • Namecheap — business email hosting.
  • GitHub — website hosting for this site.
  • Google Analytics — website usage statistics, where visitors have consented.
  • Professional advisers, accountants or insurers where necessary and lawful.

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulatory obligations or legal proceedings.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we take appropriate steps to ensure suitable data protection arrangements are in place.

We do not share confidential employee information with third parties for unrelated purposes.

8. International data transfers

Our business email service is hosted in a UK data centre.

Some other service providers, including website-related services, may process or access personal information outside the UK.

Where international transfers are subject to UK GDPR transfer restrictions, we will ensure an appropriate transfer mechanism and any necessary safeguards are in place.

Further information about applicable safeguards can be requested using the contact details in this policy.

9. Cookies and website analytics

Our website uses cookies to support essential functionality and, where you agree, to understand how visitors use the website.

Essential cookies

These help the website function correctly and remember your cookie preferences. We use a preference cookie (cc_analytics) to record whether you have accepted or rejected optional analytics cookies.

Analytics cookies

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand website usage, such as pages visited, approximate location, device type and visitor activity. Analytics cookies are only enabled where you select Accept analytics. You can choose Essential only if you do not wish to allow analytics cookies.

You can withdraw your consent by clearing your browser cookies, which will allow you to make a new choice when the cookie banner appears again.

For more information about Google’s processing of information, visit https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Our website may also use Google Fonts, which can involve connections to Google’s services when pages are displayed.

10. How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, taking account of legal obligations, contractual arrangements and legitimate business requirements.

  • Enquiries and business correspondence — retained while relevant to the enquiry or business relationship and deleted when no longer reasonably required.
  • Client and financial records — retained for the duration of the business relationship and any further period required for accounting, tax, legal or legitimate business purposes.
  • Employee information processed for clients — retained in accordance with the client’s instructions, agreed data protection arrangements and applicable legal requirements. At the end of the relevant services, information will normally be returned or securely deleted, subject to any lawful retention requirements.
  • Website analytics — retained in accordance with our Google Analytics settings.
  • Cookie preferences — normally retained for up to 12 months.

Where information is retained for legal or regulatory purposes, we will restrict its use and retain it only for as long as necessary.

11. Your data protection rights

Under UK GDPR, individuals have certain rights concerning their personal information, subject to applicable conditions and exemptions. These include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information in certain circumstances.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Exercise rights relating to certain solely automated decisions, where applicable.

To exercise your rights in relation to information controlled by Crawford Consultancy, please email hello@crawfordconsultancy.co.uk.

We will respond within the applicable statutory timeframe, normally one month, subject to any lawful extension.

Where information is processed on behalf of a client, we may refer the request to the relevant data controller and provide appropriate assistance.

12. Complaints

If you have concerns about how Crawford Consultancy handles personal information, please contact us at hello@crawfordconsultancy.co.uk. We welcome the opportunity to address concerns directly.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator: https://ico.org.uk.

You do not need to contact us before raising a complaint with the ICO.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, business practices or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on our website with an updated revision date.
