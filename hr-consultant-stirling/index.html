Employment law compliance for Stirling employers

Employment law changes regularly, and for a small business owner it can be hard to keep up. From the introduction of day-one rights for workers to the Fair Work Act Scotland and changes to TUPE regulations, there’s a lot to navigate. We keep on top of it so you don’t have to, and we’ll tell you what actually matters for your business rather than overwhelming you with detail.

We help Stirling businesses get their employment documentation right from the start — employment contracts, staff handbooks, GDPR-compliant HR records and clear disciplinary procedures — so that if anything does go wrong, you’re protected.

Payroll services for Stirling businesses
