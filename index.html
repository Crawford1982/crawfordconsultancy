About

Support from someone who understands small businesses

Having spent many years working in family businesses and operational roles, I know first-hand how much small business owners have to juggle. When you’re wearing lots of hats, it’s easy for things to get put off — but with employment law continuing to change, and the introduction of the Fair Work Agency, it’s important to stay on top of the people side of your business.

That’s where I come in. With a background spanning operations, HR and payroll, I offer flexible, affordable support to help small businesses stay on top of HR, payroll, benefits, training and business admin — without the cost of employing in-house.

Services

Support shaped around your business

Support can be one-off, ongoing or project-based — depending on what your business needs.

HR Support

Practical HR support for every stage of the employee journey — from recruitment and onboarding to contracts, policies and day-to-day people management.

  • Recruitment and interview support
  • Contracts, policies and handbooks
  • Induction, onboarding and employee paperwork
  • Day-to-day HR support, including absence and employee-related matters
Payroll

End-to-end payroll support to help keep everything running smoothly behind the scenes.

  • Weekly or monthly payroll processing
  • Timesheets, time and attendance, overtime and commissions
  • Statutory payments, starters, leavers and payroll changes
  • Payroll admin, including change of terms letters and employee paperwork
Benefits

Support with the admin that sits behind employee benefits, helping keep things organised and up to date.

  • Benefits administration and employee changes
  • Cycle to work, refer a friend and similar schemes
  • Benefit nominations and employee forms
  • General support with employee benefits and records
Training

Practical training to help managers and teams feel more confident, informed and supported.

  • HR and people management training
  • Policy and process-based training
  • Absence, disciplinary and grievance training
  • Leadership training support as your business grows
Business Support

Extra support with the admin and practical side of running a busy business.

  • Tender and procurement support
  • General admin support and business coordination
  • Process reviews and improvements
  • Forms, documents and business paperwork
