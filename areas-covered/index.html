Central Scotland

Supporting small businesses across Central Scotland

Small businesses often need support that feels practical, flexible and affordable — not overcomplicated or overly corporate.

Crawford Consultancy works with businesses across Central Scotland, offering support with everything from employment contracts and policies to payroll, recruitment, onboarding, employee matters and general business admin support.

Some clients prefer face-to-face support at their premises, while others are happy to work remotely by phone, email or video call. Both options are available, depending on the type of support you need and what works best for your business.

How It Works

On-site or remote support

Some businesses like having someone come out to site, particularly when support involves onboarding, employee meetings, training or getting to know the business properly. Others prefer the flexibility of remote support for day-to-day advice, payroll or smaller pieces of work.

Crawford Consultancy offers both on-site and remote support, so the approach can be shaped around what suits your business best.
