We offer employment contracts, staff handbooks, HR policies, absence management, disciplinary and grievance guidance, and day-to-day people advice. We also handle weekly and monthly payroll, pension administration and HMRC compliance.

Why use a local HR consultant in Falkirk?

Falkirk’s business community includes retail, professional services, logistics, manufacturing and growing local employers who need practical support that fits how they actually operate. We’re happy to meet in person locally or work remotely — whichever suits you.
